CHAPTER 01
Voice Artist Kya Hota Hai? (Mindset & Foundation)

1. Ye Kya Hai?
Voice Artist koi aisa insaan nahi hota jo sirf sundar ya bhaari aawaaz mein text padh deta hai. Voice Artist ek actor hota hai jo bina screen par aaye sirf apni aawaaz ki madad se characters aur emotions ko zinda karta hai.

Golden Rule: Acting First, Voice Second. Awaaz ek instrument hai, par use bajane wala tumhara emotion hai.

Practical Exercise:
Apne phone ka recorder on karo aur is line ko do alag tareeqon se bolo:
"Mujhe pata tha tum zaroor aaoge... lekin itni der kar doge, ye nahi socha tha."
