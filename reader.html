CHAPTER 01
Voice Artist Kya Hota Hai? (Foundational Mindset)

1. Ye Kya Hai?
Voice Artist koi aisa shaks nahi hota jo sirf script ko bhaari ya meethi aawaaz me padh de. Voice artist ek unseen actor hota hai jo bina camera ke samne aaye sirf apni saans aur aawaaz se kahani aur jazbaat create karta hai.

Golden Rule: Acting First, Voice Second. Awaaz ek instrument hai, aur tumhara emotion use bajane wala.

Practical Drill:
Apne phone ka recorder on karo aur is line ko do alag tareeqon se bolo:
"Mujhe pata tha tum zaroor aaoge... lekin itni der kar doge, ye nahi socha tha."
Take 1: Bina kisi feel ke seedha padho.
Take 2: Ek dost ke aane ki ummeed aur thehrav ke sath bolo.
